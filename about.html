ℹ️ About AI Doctor Analysis

Our mission, vision, and the technology behind your health companion.

🎯

Our Mission

To make accurate, science-backed health information accessible to everyone — regardless of location, income, or education level.

👁️

Our Vision

A world where every person has the knowledge to make informed decisions about their health before visiting a doctor.

🔬

Our Approach

We curate health data from trusted medical sources and present it in a simple, understandable format for everyday people.

🔒

Privacy First

We do not collect or store any personal health data. All symptom checks happen locally in your browser, completely private.

How It Works

Simple steps to use AI Doctor Analysis

1

Enter Your Symptoms

Type one or more symptoms into the symptom checker on the home page (e.g., "fever", "cough", "rash").

2

Get Matched Conditions

Our local database matches your symptoms to possible conditions, ranked by how closely they align.

3

Learn & Explore

Read detailed information about each condition — causes, treatments, and prevention strategies.

4

Consult a Doctor

Use this information to have a more informed conversation with your healthcare provider. Never self-diagnose.
